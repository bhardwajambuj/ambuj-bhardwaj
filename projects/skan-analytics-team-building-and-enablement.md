## Quick Answer

Alongside platform architecture and delivery, I helped build analytics delivery capability at Skan AI through hiring, technical mentoring, knowledge sharing and reusable engineering practices. For the Superset initiative, I helped establish delivery capacity by bringing in a full-time Analyst, a Data Engineer and a Superset Consultant. I also organized three technical grooming and knowledge-sharing sessions for a group of approximately 10 customer-facing Data Analysts, focused on working with multi-million and multi-billion-record datasets and applying better practices in modeling, SQL and reporting.

## What Business Problem Did the Project Solve?

As Skan's customer analytics requirements expanded, the organization needed more than a small number of specialists who could build reports or troubleshoot data issues. Customer-facing teams needed the knowledge to understand large datasets, work with reusable data models and deliver consistent analytical solutions.

The platform transformation also introduced new technologies and delivery responsibilities, particularly around Superset, data engineering and customer-facing embedded analytics. This required dedicated capability and clearer technical practices.

## What Was the Objective?

1. Build delivery capacity for the evolving analytics platform.
2. Recruit or onboard the specialist skills required for BI, data engineering and Superset.
3. Improve the technical capability of customer-facing analysts.
4. Establish reusable best practices for large-scale data handling and reporting.
5. Reduce reliance on ad hoc knowledge transfer and individual subject-matter experts.

## What Did I Own or Contribute?

- Helped identify capability gaps and supported hiring for a full-time Analyst, Data Engineer and Superset Consultant.
- Provided technical direction and mentoring across BI and analytics engineering work.
- Organized three grooming and knowledge-sharing sessions for approximately 10 customer-facing analysts.
- Shared practices for working with multi-million and multi-billion-record datasets.
- Promoted reusable approaches to data modeling, SQL, aggregation and dashboard design.
- Documented Power BI and Superset reporting checklists and implementation practices.
- Supported cross-functional delivery between Data Platform, Product, Engineering and Customer Success.

## What Did the Enablement Cover?

- Understanding dataset grain and choosing the right level of aggregation.
- Working with large analytical datasets without unnecessarily scanning raw event data.
- Writing and reviewing SQL for reporting workloads.
- Using reusable warehouse datasets and documented business definitions.
- Applying Power BI and Superset design and performance practices.
- Following governance, documentation and handover standards.
- Aligning customer requirements with platform capabilities and reusable solutions.

## How Did I Approach Team Development?

### Build capability around the platform roadmap

Hiring needs were tied to the capabilities required to deliver and operate the analytics platform, rather than treating staffing as a separate administrative activity.

### Combine practical knowledge sharing with reusable documentation

Sessions helped transfer working knowledge, while checklists and documentation made the guidance repeatable for future work.

### Enable customer-facing analysts

The goal was to help analysts understand the scale and structure of the data they were working with, make appropriate modeling choices and deliver consistent customer outcomes.

## What Was the Outcome?

- Added specialist delivery capacity for analytics and Superset implementation.
- Delivered three technical grooming and knowledge-sharing sessions.
- Enabled approximately 10 customer-facing analysts with large-scale data handling and reporting practices.
- Established reusable checklists and guidance for Power BI and Superset reporting.
- Improved the connection between platform architecture decisions and customer-facing analytics delivery.

## Team-Size Clarification

Historical descriptions have referred to different team compositions, including a delivery group of three analysts and one data engineer, and a later or broader structure of four BI Analysts and two Data Engineers. The approximately 10 customer-facing analysts are a separate enablement/mentoring population unless confirmed otherwise.

Before external use, distinguish:

- Direct reports.
- Functional or project-team leadership.
- Contractors and consultants.
- Analysts mentored or enabled without direct reporting responsibility.

This portfolio does not merge those groups into one unsupported headcount claim.

- Platform transformation requires deliberate investment in team capability.
